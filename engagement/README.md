# Engagement kit

Working documents used before, during and after an authorized security
assessment. They are templates: fill the bracketed fields, have both parties
sign, and keep the signed copy with the engagement record.

| Document | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Scoping questionnaire](scoping-questionnaire.md) | Before quoting | Establish targets, ownership, constraints and success criteria |
| [Rules of engagement](rules-of-engagement.md) | Before any testing | Written authorization, scope, limits, contacts, data handling |
| [Operating standard](operating-standard.md) | Always | Security and data-handling controls the assessor must meet |
| [Sample report](../reports/sample-assessment-report.md) | After | Format and evidence standard of the deliverable |

These are practitioner templates, not legal advice. Have counsel review the
rules of engagement before first use in your jurisdiction; in Brazil, unauthorized
access is a crime under Lei 12.737/2012 (Código Penal art. 154-A) and personal
data handling falls under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018), so written authorization
from the system owner is the one document that is never optional.
